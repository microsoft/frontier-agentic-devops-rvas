# Ch43: Repository Inventory Cleanup Delivery Assurance

Apply the [Delivery Assurance Standard](../../../DELIVERY_ASSURANCE.md); `README.md` is canonical.

## Assurance record

- **Authorized scope:** record organization, inventory cohort, approver, and cleanup owner.
- **Evidence:** attach the inventory export with cleanup decisions, gap analysis, metadata changes, and deferred high-impact actions.
- **Open risk:** record unowned repositories, contested duplicates, pending approvals, or `none`.
- **Next decision:** record next cleanup cohort, owner, and review cadence.

## Session-specific reviewer focus

- Confirm that the inventory supports ownership and cleanup decisions.
- **Governance controls:** Verify that setup does not archive, delete, or transfer repositories.
- Ask how teams escalate missing owners and resolve repositories with duplicate purposes.
- Ask which safe metadata change was applied and where the inventory lives.
