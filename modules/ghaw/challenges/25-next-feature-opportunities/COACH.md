# Next Feature Opportunities Agent — Delivery Assurance

Review the completed work against the
[Delivery Assurance Standard](../../../DELIVERY_ASSURANCE.md) and the paired
`README.md` Definition of Done.

## Assurance record

- **Authorized scope:** record the selected product repository and approving
  product owner.
- **Evidence:** retain the compiled workflow, dry-run result, and first
  recommendation issue or no-op output.
- **Open risk:** record the risk of weak evidence, generic recommendations, or
  backlog noise and the accountable owner.
- **Next decision:** record which recommendation, if any, becomes planned work
  and who owns the decision.

## Session-specific reviewer focus

- Confirm that every reported opportunity is tied to repository evidence and is
  not an open issue restated by the agent.
- Confirm that the workflow uses no write permission and only the constrained
  issue safe output.
