# Tone of voice

**Voice:** Direct, with clear-thinker refinements.

Write like a senior engineer explaining something to another competent engineer.
Use short, direct sentences, ordinary words, and explicit actors when they matter.
Lead with the main point. Remove bureaucracy, repetition, and low-value detail.
Keep the facts, qualifications, product names, and technical terminology intact.
Use bold text where it helps the reader find the point.
