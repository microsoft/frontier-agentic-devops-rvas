# Delivery Assurance Standard

Delivery assurance verifies that a customer-owned outcome is safe to adopt. It reviews the delivery guide; it does not replace or repeat it.

## Record four things

1. **Authorized scope:** The customer tenant, repository, service, or migration wave, plus the customer owner who approved it.
2. **Evidence:** Links or locations for the evidence behind each completed item in the delivery guide's Definition of Done.
3. **Open risk:** Each unresolved constraint, its control, and the person accountable for it. Record "none" when no material risk remains.
4. **Next decision:** The handover, pilot, rollout, cutover, or follow-up action, with its owner and target date.

## Review principles

- The delivery guide is the canonical source for tasks, acceptance criteria, commands, and troubleshooting. Do not copy its checklist here.
- Inspect evidence independently when practical. A checked task is not enough if the team cannot show the outcome, scope, or control.
- Use a customer-owned environment whenever authorized. A sample environment is evidence of a pattern, not evidence of customer adoption.
- Keep assurance guidance to exceptions. Record only controls or risks specific to the product, tenant, or migration. Put general teaching material in the facilitator notes.

## Completion

The reviewer accepts the outcome when the evidence supports the guide's Definition of Done, all four records are complete, and the customer owner agrees to the next decision.
