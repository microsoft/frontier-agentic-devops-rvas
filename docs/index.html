The experience

How it works.

Your team does the work in its own environment by following one practical delivery guide.

01
Pick an activity

Browse the catalog and filter it to fit your team. Each card shows the time, difficulty, and background you will need.

02
Set up your environment

Run the setup script in your organization. It creates the repositories and other resources used by the activity.

03
Do the work

Follow the delivery guide with your team and keep the result in your own environment.

04
Review the result

Review what changed, record the useful evidence, and keep the result with the team.
